About

Profile

Starting from the market and the customer, I define the technical challenge and translate research into products and businesses for the appliance, electronics and automotive industries.

With a Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering and a Professional MBA from KAIST, my career spans R&D, manufacturing technology, product marketing and business development. At SABIC, I lead materials projects for the appliance and E&E industries across Asia-Pacific.
